## Limitations

- 18 mutants of one app (TodoMVC). Every bug is a single synthetic mutation, so these numbers say nothing about other apps or about bugs that span several files.
- TRUE_FIX means the differential fuzzer found no behavior difference from the unmutated app. That is strong evidence, not a proof of equivalence.
- A clean-video false fix counts every claimed fix on a video without a bug, including patches that do not change behavior.
- Latency is wall time measured while several runs share one machine and one API quota.
- With 18 mutants each run moves a rate by 6 points or more.
- Decision model calls are priced at $0 because the price is unknown, so Glint's cost is a lower bound.
